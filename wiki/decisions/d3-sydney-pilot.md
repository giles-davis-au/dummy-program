# D3 — Pilot in Sydney only before national rollout

**Decision:** Launch will pilot in Sydney only, with a 10-merchant cohort, before any
national rollout decision is made.

**Decided by:** Giles Davis, Ben Okafor — at the 2026-08-14 weekly sync.

**Rationale:** a single-city pilot reduces risk before committing to a national
rollout — keeps the blast radius small while validating the credit engine and
support processes end to end.

Corroborated by the meeting notes doc, the RAID log's Decisions table, and Giles's
same-day Slack post in #flexpay-programme.

This decision is the basis for milestone **M08 — Pilot merchant cohort live (10
merchants, Sydney)**, due 2026-10-20 — see [flexpay-au.md](../projects/flexpay-au.md).
