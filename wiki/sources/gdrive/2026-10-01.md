<a id="gdoc-20260812-sync"></a>
### 2026-08-12 — Weekly Sync
Attendees: Giles Davis, Dan Foster, Owen Mackay, Isla Novak, Priya Shah.

> Owen raised concerns about the auditability of a fully automated ML credit decision
> model for the AUSTRAC assessment — a rules-based approach with a manual review
> queue would be far easier to explain and evidence. Isla noted Finance would also
> prefer a more conservative, better-understood risk exposure profile at initial
> launch scale, given this is entirely on Money Ltd's own balance sheet with no
> external credit partner to share exposure. Agreed to defer the ML model to a
> post-launch iteration.

Decisions: **D2 (supersedes D1)** — Launch with a simpler, explainable rules-based
credit decision model plus a manual review queue for edge cases; defer the ML-based
model to a post-launch iteration. Decided by Giles Davis, Owen Mackay, Isla Novak.

Actions: Priya to update PDS drafting assumptions to reflect the new model approach;
Dan to re-scope M02 engineering work around the rules-based approach.
