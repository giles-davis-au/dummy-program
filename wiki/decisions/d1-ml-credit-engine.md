# D1 — Build an in-house ML-based credit decision engine

**Decision:** Build a fully automated, real-time ML-based credit decision engine
in-house for launch.

**Decided by:** Giles Davis, Dan Foster — at the 2026-07-10 programme kickoff /
weekly sync.
[sources/gdrive/2026-10-01.md#gdoc-20260710-kickoff](../sources/gdrive/2026-10-01.md#gdoc-20260710-kickoff)

**Status: superseded by [D2 — rules-based credit model](d2-rules-based-credit-model.md)
on 2026-08-12.** Compliance (Owen Mackay) raised concerns that a fully automated ML
model would be hard to audit and explain for the AUSTRAC AML/CTF assessment; Finance
(Isla Novak) separately wanted a more conservative, better-understood risk exposure
profile given the programme is funded entirely off Money Ltd's own balance sheet with
no external credit partner. See
[d2-rules-based-credit-model.md](d2-rules-based-credit-model.md) for the replacement
decision and full rationale.
